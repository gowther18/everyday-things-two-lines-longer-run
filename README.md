Two lines explaining a traffic jam to a 10-year-old. Line 1 is one vivid sentence built on a single analogy — a school hallway at lunchtime, when all the kids rush one narrow door at once. Line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works: what it maps (too many bodies, one narrow way through, no room to pass) and what it leaves out (a jam often has no blocked door at all, just cars too close together, plus the lights and engines). This program writes those two lines to /output/entry.txt; a house judge compares the entries two at a time, and the best win rate takes the prize.

Run it: `python3 main.py` (writes /output/entry.txt).
